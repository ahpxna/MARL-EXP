## E12 — when is the zero-byte emulator (EMU_OLD) exact?

**Object.** EMU_OLD is `ExactVO(packet_only=True, rule='older')`, exactly E7's arm.

How it works:
* The public legacy mirror gives, for each OLD receiver i and sender j, the measurement time m*_ij(t) that the K1 view holds.
* EMU looks up a copy of j measured at m*_ij(t) among the copies i received on U2. It accepts a copy received directly, or relayed at age < 14 steps, so that the 4-bit age recovers the time.
* Otherwise EMU substitutes the newest usable OLDER copy.

**Coverage.** C(i,t) holds iff, for every j with m*_ij(t) defined, the key (j, m*_ij(t)) is usable by i at t.

**E12-a (theorem; Lean module LeanMarlPacketEmulatorV1, written after this freeze).** Assumptions:
* reference entries are honest: the payload is the sender's measurement at its measurement time — proved for the K1 model as `ref_honest` in LeanMarlLegacyViewDecoderV1;
* the receiver's store holds only honest copies.

Under these assumptions the emulated view at (i,t) equals the K1 view at (i,t) iff C(i,t). This holds under the rules `older` and `absent`; under `fresh` coverage is only sufficient.

**E12-b (implementation and theorem check; per OLD agent-step).** A new evaluator `scripts/MARL_e3_emu_coverage_eval.py` runs:
* the E7 mixed fleet with OLD agents acting on EMU_OLD;
* a lockstep K1 reference environment, stepped with the same joint actions and used for checks only.

It records, per step and per OLD agent:
* coverage;
* class of each missing key: `never` (no copy of that measurement ever received) or `ambiguous` (received only at age ≥ 14);
* view equality: full signature including measurement time, and actor-visible (sender, age, payload);
* input equality;
* action equality: the OLD policy on the EMU view vs on the reference view.

Prediction: view equality (full signature) ⇔ C(i,t) on every OLD agent-step — 0 exceptions in either direction.

**E12-c (episode level).** Two predictions:
* this evaluator's queue = E7's `U2/EMU_OLD` queue on every (seed, layout, episode) — implementation consistency;
* on every episode with no OLD coverage failure, E7's `U2/EMU_OLD` queue = E7's `U2/EXACT_PROTO_OLD` queue.

**E12-d (descriptive, no threshold).** Report:
* failure rate per OLD agent-step;
* class shares;
* failures by step t and by number of NEW neighbours;
* P(action mismatch | failure);
* share of episodes with ≥ 1 failure;
* EMU − EXACT queue difference conditional on failures.

Weak prior (from E6 and P2): failure rate ≤ 1 % of OLD agent-steps, ambiguous share 0.

**Panel.** E7 panel 7501–7564 × headway 8/16; layouts D and C; actor seeds 0–9. Dev smoke: traffic 901, seed 0.

**Self-checks** (no manifest otherwise):
* first episode, layout ALL: queue = the homogeneous packet-only run (`MARL_e3_emulator_rules_eval` EXACT_PACKET), as in E7;
* physical identity of the two environments at every step;

## E5-G3 — out-of-support mass vs weighted out-of-support mass on the C0 exact tables

**Inputs.** A FROZEN rerun of the C0 exact Bayes tables and evaluation panels by the current `scripts/CITY_c0_testbed.py` (sha256 236c2ebebb7e1b8d…, = commit 4e8fe126, `--panels`, sizes 3000 / 3000 / 1000 / 8):
* lights FT, ACT, EXT, CUT, A1–A6 → `research/city_sim/c0_g3_frozen_20261004/panels/`;
* reported, not required: whether the rerun's tables and panels are byte-identical to `c0_v3_20261002/panels`, which bears on C-9's provenance gap for the tables.

**Controllers and pairs.**
* Controllers are the exact Z tables Z*_T, one per training light T (10 controllers; no student training noise).
* Pairs are (T, D) with T ≠ D among the 10 lights: 90 pairs.
* Target: Δ(T, D) = mean regret(Z*_T on D's panel) − mean regret(Z*_D on D's panel), the mismatch penalty of C0-1.

**Predictors.** Each is computed from the decision steps of Z*_T driving on D's panel, per decision step. Out of support (OOS) means τ ≥ hi_T, the longest phase of T: the table clips τ to hi_T − 1, where its hazard is 1.

| Predictor | Definition | Deployable? |
|---|---|---|
| RAW | Share of OOS steps | Yes |
| SENS | Share of OOS steps at which the controller's action at (x, v, colour) is NOT constant over τ ∈ [0, hi_T − 1] (the decision depends on τ inside its support) | Yes (controller-only) |
| STAKES | Mean over steps of 1[OOS] · (max_a Q_T − min_a Q_T) at the clipped state (cost at stake by the controller's own values) | Yes (controller-only) |
| ORACLE | Mean over steps of 1[OOS] · A_D(s, a_T), the advantage loss under D's own exact table restricted to OOS steps. Uses the deployment light's optimal table. | No — diagnostic |

Over ALL steps (not only OOS), mean Σ A_D equals mean cost(Z*_T on D) − mean V_D(s0) by the performance-difference identity. This is a SELF-CHECK, not a predictor: the residual is reported, and the run fails if any pair with |Δ| > 1 has relative residual > 10 %.

**Fit / test.** Controllers are split by position in the canonical list:
* H1 = {FT, EXT, A1, A3, A5}
* H2 = {ACT, CUT, A2, A4, A6}

Procedure, in both directions:
1. On the fit half, choose the better deployable weighting (SENS or STAKES) by Spearman with Δ over that half's 45 pairs.
2. On the held-out half, compare Spearman(chosen, Δ) with Spearman(RAW, Δ).

**G3a SUPPORTED** iff, in BOTH directions, held-out Spearman(chosen) > Spearman(RAW), and the bootstrap (panel episodes resampled within each D, 1000 draws) lower bound of the difference is > 0.

Also reported: Kendall τ; ORACLE's Spearman as an upper reference; and the share of Δ out of support, ORACLE / Δ, per pair. In-support harm (the CUT-type case) is where RAW must fail.

**Script.** `scripts/CITY_g3_oos_weighting.py` → `research/city_sim/g3_oos_weighting_20261004/` (report.txt, result.json, manifest with sha256 of the testbed, panels and tables).

## E5-G6 — sequential "find one adequate upgrade" on Q7b's rows (offline)

**Data.** `research/city_sim/q7b_contract_20261002/eval_t{0..3}.json` (420 rows, producer sha recorded in C-8).

Candidates per training seed t: the 9 non-ACT lights FT, RL-H, RL-J, RL-C30, RL-C40, RL-C50, RL-C60, RL-C80, RL-C120.

Per evaluation seed s ∈ {100..104}, paired against ACT:
* light gain g_s = timeloss(ACT, human, s) − timeloss(U, human, s);
* fleet regression r_s = approach_cost(L-ACT | U, s) − approach_cost(L-ACT | ACT, s).

A "paid rollout" = one (candidate, seed) evaluation, i.e. one human-traffic run + one fleet run. Full evaluation = 9 × 5 = 45 per t.

**Adequacy.** g ≥ γ and r ≤ ε, with γ = 5 % of mean timeloss(ACT, human) and ε = 5 % of mean approach_cost(L-ACT | ACT), per t.
* Truth = the full-evaluation means over the 5 seeds.
* Sensitivity grid: γ ∈ {2.5, 5, 7.5} %, ε ∈ {2.5, 5, 10} %.

**Procedure** (racing / successive elimination):
* Each round, every surviving candidate is evaluated on one more seed, in a random permutation of its 5 seeds.
* After each round (n ≥ 2), two-sided t-bounds are computed at level α / (2 · 9 · 4): union bound over the two quantities, 9 candidates and the 4 looks n = 2…5.
* Eliminate a candidate if UCB(g) < γ or LCB(r) > ε.
* ACCEPT the candidate with the highest mean g among those with LCB(g) ≥ γ and UCB(r) ≤ ε; stop at the first acceptance.
* Stop with "none" if all are eliminated or seeds are exhausted.
* α = 0.05; 2000 simulated permutation orders per t.
* Second procedure (reported): the same with `ordered` candidates — RL-H first, then caps from 120 down to 30, then RL-J, FT — evaluated one at a time to n = 5 or a decision.

**Metrics** (per t and pooled):
* mean paid rollouts / 45;
* acceptance rate;
* false acceptance (accepted candidate inadequate by truth);
* missed (truth has an adequate candidate, the test accepts none);
* retained gain = g_true(accepted) / max g_true over truly adequate candidates (0 if missed; undefined if none adequate).

Reference: full evaluation with the same t-bounds at n = 5 (the "certified full" decision) and with point estimates.

**GO for a live run** (registered rule of E5): pooled mean paid rollouts ≤ 60 % of full, AND false acceptance ≤ 5 %, AND mean retained gain ≥ 80 % over t with an adequate candidate. Otherwise NO-GO: offline shows no saving.

**Script.** `scripts/CITY_g6_sequential_upgrade.py` → `research/city_sim/g6_sequential_20261004/`.
