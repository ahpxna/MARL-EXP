## 1. Statement

Model as handed to this worker. Query `e` observed as `Y_e ~ N(m_theta(e) + b*v_e, sigma_e^2)`
with `b in R` an unknown common nuisance; two worlds give mean difference `d_e`; exposure
`w_e`, `alpha_e = w_e/sigma_e^2`; profiled information
`I_prof(w) = (1/2) inf_u sum_e alpha_e (d_e - u v_e)^2`.

**N6.1 (identity).** With `alpha_e >= 0` and `S_dd, S_dv, S_vv` the `alpha`-weighted inner
products, `sum_e alpha_e (d_e - u v_e)^2 = S_vv (u - S_dv/S_vv)^2 + (S_dd - S_dv^2/S_vv)`
whenever `S_vv > 0`, so `I_prof = (1/2)(S_dd - S_dv^2/S_vv)`. If `S_vv = 0` then `alpha >= 0`
plus Cauchy–Schwarz *forces* `S_dv = 0`, the objective is the constant `S_dd`, and
`I_prof = (1/2) S_dd`. The two cases are exhaustive, and no division by zero ever arises.
Non-negativity of `alpha` is load-bearing, not decorative: at `alpha = (1,-1)`, `v = (1,1)`,
`d = (1,0)` one has `S_vv = 0` and `S_dv = 1`, and the infimum is `-infinity`.

**N6.2 (immediate negative result).** With `v_e = 1` and a single sampled query type,
`I_prof = 0` exactly, for every exposure and every `d != 0`. More generally, with `v == 1`,
`I_prof = (1/2) * (alpha-weighted variance of d)`, so it is `0` **iff `d` is constant on the
sampled support** — the single-query case is the extreme instance. Repeating one query
forever does not separate a world change from an offset change.

**N6.3 (design duality — the correct headline).** Budget `sum_e kappa_e x_e = 1`, `x >= 0`,
`alpha_e = x_e/sigma_e^2`. Then

```
max_x I_prof(x)  =  (1/2) * min_{u in R} max_e (d_e - u v_e)^2 / (kappa_e sigma_e^2)
```

and the known-offset optimum is the same expression evaluated at `u = 0`,
`max_x I_known = (1/2) max_e d_e^2/(kappa_e sigma_e^2)`. Writing `s_e = sigma_e sqrt(kappa_e)`,
`g_e = d_e/s_e`, `h_e = v_e/s_e`, the **exact cost of unknown calibration** is

```
R  =  max_x I_known / max_x I_prof  =  ||g||_inf^2 / dist_inf(g, R*h)^2 ,
```

a Chebyshev-approximation ratio. The optimal design never needs more than two distinct
query types (1-D Helly on the intervals `{u : |g_e - u h_e| <= t}`).

**N6.4 (two-channel construction).** For `d = (0, gamma)`, `v = (1,1)`, costs `kappa_i`,
noises `sigma_i`, N6.3 reduces to
`max_x I_prof = gamma^2 / (2 (sigma_0 sqrt(kappa_0) + sigma_1 sqrt(kappa_1))^2)`, attained at
`x_i* = (sigma_i/sqrt(kappa_i)) / (sigma_0 sqrt(kappa_0) + sigma_1 sqrt(kappa_1))`. With equal
`sigma` and equal cost this is `gamma^2/(8 sigma^2)` against the known-offset one-channel
`gamma^2/(2 sigma^2)` — **factor 4, for these two designs only**. Unequal `(sigma, kappa)`
already move it: the factor is `(sigma_0 sqrt(k_0) + sigma_1 sqrt(k_1))^2 / (sigma_1^2 k_1)`
(observed 6.25 at `sigma_0 sqrt(k_0)=3`, `sigma_1 sqrt(k_1)=2`).

**N6.5 (the supervisor's warning, confirmed and sharpened).** With `v == 1`, equal `sigma`
and equal cost, `max_x I_prof = (max_e d_e - min_e d_e)^2 / 8` — a **range**, not a maximum —
so `R = 4 max_e d_e^2 / (range d)^2`. Consequently **4 is neither universal nor an upper
bound**:

| available contrasts `d` | `R` |
|---|---|
| `(0, gamma)` control + target | **4** |
| `(+gamma, -gamma)` balanced | **1** (unknown calibration is free) |
| `(+gamma, -gamma/2)` | 16/9 |
| `(gamma, gamma/2)` one-sided | 16 |
| `(gamma, 9gamma/10)` one-sided | **400** |
| `(gamma, gamma)` constant | **infinite** (`I_prof = 0`) |

**N6.6 (D6 legal query family — the realisability question, answered).** Transcribing
`gate`, `others`, `swapped`, `meanOffset`, `meanSlope` and `Legal` from the Lean sources and
re-deriving the affine mean law by brute-force enumeration of the Boolean cube (`q = 2, 3`;
`sigma_e^2 = 1` for every legal query, from the Lean Gaussian model; `kappa_e = 1` assumed):

* **Swap alternative** `(A, mu)` vs `(swapped A p r, mu)`, margin held fixed: the legal
  family *does* contain genuinely realisable opposite-sign pairs. Singletons `.inl p` and
  `.inl r` carry `d = +mu` and `-mu`; joint queries `(G, h)` carry `([r in G] - [p in G])(1-mu)`,
  so `(G ∋ p)` and its image under the transposition `(p r)` carry `∓(1-mu)`. The exact
  cost factor is **`R = 1` at every `mu` tested (`0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1, 7/5`) and both `q`**:
  unknown common calibration costs *nothing at all* here. Mechanism: the transposition
  `tau = (p r)` maps the legal family to itself, preserves `sigma` and `v`, and negates `d`;
  any `tau`-symmetric design has `sum_e alpha_e d_e v_e = 0`.
* **Blind design.** The uniform-over-singletons allocation satisfies `sum alpha d v = 0`
  for *all* `q(q+1)` swaps simultaneously (checked exactly). Because the blind problem is
  invariant under `Sym(coords \ {j})`, its known-offset minimax optimum can be taken
  exchangeable, hence `tau`-symmetric for every swap, hence nuisance-free; so the blind
  minimax values with and without a known offset coincide. (Symmetrization argument,
  pen-and-paper; see §5 for what is and is not certified.)
* **Margin alternative** `(A, mu)` vs `(A, nu)`, hidden set fixed: here the argmax face
  *is* sign-definite (all the loudest queries have slope `-q`), and the exact factor is
  `R = 4q^2/(1+q)^2` — `16/9 = 1.7778` at `q = 2`, `9/4 = 2.25` at `q = 3`. It approaches
  4 only as `q -> infinity` and is never 4 at finite `q`.
* **Mixed alternative** (swap *and* margin move together): the cancellation breaks —
  coordinates outside `A` pick up `(nu - mu)`, so `tau` no longer negates `d`. Observed
  `R = 1.3061` and `1.5625`. This is the scope boundary of the free-calibration result.
* **Restricted subfamily.** Restricting the design to the two loudest legal queries
  (slopes `-q` and `-(q-1)`) gives exactly `R = 4q^2` — 16, 36, 100, 256 at `q = 2,3,5,8`.
  These are legal D6 queries; an acquisition policy that happens to use only the loudest
  channels pays `4q^2`, not 4.

**N6.7 (the shifted-reference construction contains its own mirror channel).** In the fixed
world `F_{A,t0}(x) = g_A(x) - E_{t0}[g_A] + sum_i (rho 1_T(i) - s_i(t0))(x_i - t0)` with readout
`Delta_i(t1)`, for the two-point test `F_{A,t0}` vs `F_{A',t0}` (`A' = A` with `p` swapped for
`r`), **exactly two coordinates move, and they move by `+Gamma` and `-Gamma`**, with
`Gamma = (1-t1)^(2k-2) - (1-t0)^(2k-2)` (verified against the cube at `k = 3, 4`, three
`(t0,t1)` pairs each; `Gamma = 0` exactly at `t1 = t0`). So the companion channel of the
shifted-reference query is **not** forced to be a `d = 0` control: the mirror coordinate `r`
is a query of the same type and the same cost carrying `-Gamma`. Spending the second half of
the budget there instead of on a control doubles the contrast to `2*Gamma` and recovers the
entire factor of 4 (`R = 4` for the control design, `R = 1` for the balanced one, and the
balanced design's profiled optimum is exactly `4x` the control design's).

## 2. Assumptions

1. `alpha_e >= 0` (non-negative exposures). Dropping it breaks the two-case dichotomy of
   N6.1 and makes the infimum `-infinity`; the probe exhibits this.
2. Gaussian with **known** per-query variance `sigma_e^2`, independent across queries;
   `I_prof` is the minimum KL between the two *composite* hypotheses `{(theta_0, b)}` and
   `{(theta_1, b')}`. This is the Le Cam two-point separation, and is numerically identical
   to the Schur complement `I_thetatheta - I_thetab^2/I_bb` of the 2x2 Fisher matrix.
3. **One** scalar nuisance `b`, shared by all queries, entering additively with a *known*
   loading vector `v`. Unknown `v`, per-query offsets, or drift are outside scope.
4. Budget `sum_e kappa_e x_e = 1` with `kappa_e > 0`, `x >= 0`; exposure is continuous
   (no integrality). Neyman-style continuous allocation.
5. **D6 specifics:** `sigma_e^2 = 1` for every legal query — this is read off the Lean model
   (`gaussianReal (actualMean A j mu e) 1`, `LeanD6GaussianQueryDominanceV1.lean:108-112`),
   not assumed. **`kappa_e = 1` (one sample per legal query) IS an assumption of this probe.**
   There is no cost/pricing model for legal queries anywhere in the D6 Lean source; the only
   `kappa` in the graph is `D6DecisionInteractionComplexity.kappaDI`, an unrelated quantity.
   If real costs scale with `|G|`, every D6 number in §1 changes.
6. **D6 offset loading `v_e = 1`.** Justified for the Lean D6 query family, where one query
   is one scalar reading `Y ~ N(actualMean, 1)`. **Not** justified for the MASTER `Delta_i`
   readout: `Delta_i = E[Y|X_i=1] - E[Y|X_i=0]` is already a difference, so a strictly common
   additive `b` on the raw `Y` cancels outright (`v_e = 0`) and there is no nuisance to
   profile. Which model applies is a modelling choice, not a theorem proved here.
7. The D6 world family is the two-parameter `(A, mu)` family of
   `LeanD6ThresholdConstructionV1`; alternatives considered are swap, margin, and mixed.

## 3. Nearest prior work

  `gamma^2/(2(sigma_0 sqrt(k_0)+sigma_1 sqrt(k_1))^2)` and the exposures `x_i*` are a
  c-optimal design under a cost constraint; the `min_u max_e` form of N6.3 is the standard
  dual of c-optimality for a two-parameter model. The repo already cites Elfving

  correct standard there: "Cauchy–Schwarz or incidence balancing is not new novelty."
* **Pukelsheim, *Optimal Design of Experiments*, ch. 2** — c-optimality, Elfving sets,
  design duality. Covers N6.3 in generality.
* **Neyman / optimal cost allocation** (Statistics Canada, *Survey Methods and Practices*) —
  covers the `x_i ∝ sigma_i/sqrt(kappa_i)` rule.
* **Schur complement / profiled or effective information** — textbook; N6.1 is not novel,
  as the brief already concedes.
* **Reda, Tirinzoni, Degenne, NeurIPS 2021** (structured misspecification with matching
  sample complexity) — adjacent, but its quantities are not this linear-invariant class.
* **Nothing found in this repo** proving N6.1 or N6.3. `gq.py find profil` returns

**Residual novelty claim, stated narrowly.** Not the identity, not the Cauchy–Schwarz

not capped at 4; (b) the concrete reference/control/spatial-contrast geometry of *this*
construction — that the D6 legal family and the shifted-reference construction both contain
realisable `(+gamma, -gamma)` pairs, that the swap alternative therefore costs exactly
nothing, and that the margin alternative costs exactly `4q^2/(1+q)^2`.

## 4. Proof / counterexample

**N6.1.** Expand `sum alpha (d - uv)^2 = S_dd - 2u S_dv + u^2 S_vv`. If `S_vv > 0` complete the
square. If `S_vv = 0`, then since every `alpha_e >= 0` and `S_vv = sum alpha_e v_e^2 = 0`, every
`e` with `alpha_e > 0` has `v_e = 0`; hence `S_dv = 0` and the objective is constant. Also
`S_dv^2 <= S_dd S_vv = 0` directly. **Verified exactly** at 400 random rational configurations
(382 nondegenerate, 18 with `S_vv = 0`); the completed-square identity is checked as a
polynomial identity at four distinct rational `u` (a quadratic is pinned by three), residual
exactly 0.

**N6.2.** One query: `S_dd = alpha d^2`, `S_dv = alpha d`, `S_vv = alpha`, so
`S_dd - S_dv^2/S_vv = alpha d^2 - alpha d^2 = 0`. Independent of `alpha`, hence of exposure.
Checked at exposure up to `1e9`, `|d|` up to `1e4`, `sigma^2 in {1, 1/9, 17/3}`; residual 0.

**N6.3.** `J(x,u) = (1/2) sum_e (x_e/sigma_e^2)(d_e - u v_e)^2` is linear (hence concave) in
`x` on the compact convex budget simplex and convex in `u` on `R`; `u` may be restricted to a
compact interval without changing the infimum. Sion's minimax theorem gives
`max_x min_u J = min_u max_x J`, and `max_x` of a linear function over the simplex is the
largest vertex value, `(1/2) max_e (d_e - u v_e)^2/(kappa_e sigma_e^2)`. **Certified exactly**:
for 60 random rational instances the probe reconstructs an optimal design from the Chebyshev
minimiser (active set + profiled stationarity `sum_e y_e h_e rho_e = 0`, `y_e = kappa_e x_e`)
and checks it attains the dual value in exact rationals — 60/60, residual exactly 0. A
randomized falsification sweep (40 full-support designs per instance) never beat the dual.
Support-size-2 sufficiency verified exactly on 40 further families.

**N6.4.** Special case of N6.3 with `d = (0, gamma)`, `v = (1,1)`:
`min_u max(|u|/s_0, |gamma - u|/s_1)` is minimised at `u = s_0 gamma/(s_0+s_1)` with value
`|gamma|/(s_0+s_1)`, `s_i = sigma_i sqrt(kappa_i)`. Verified exactly on five `(kappa, sigma,
gamma)` configurations; `x*` checked to satisfy the budget and to attain the optimum, residual 0.

**N6.5 — the counterexample to "factor 4 is universal."** `d = (+gamma, -gamma)`,
`v = (1,1)`, equal `sigma` and `kappa`, `x = (1/2, 1/2)`: `S_dv = (1/2)gamma - (1/2)gamma = 0`,
so the nuisance penalty is exactly 0 and `I_prof = I_known = gamma^2/(2 sigma^2)` — the same
value a known-offset analyst achieves. `R = 1`. **Counterexample to "4 is an upper bound":**
`d = (gamma, 9gamma/10)` gives range `gamma/10`, `max|d| = gamma`, so `R = 400` exactly;
`d = (gamma, gamma)` gives `R = infinity`. All exact.

**N6.6.** From `actual_mean_affine` (`actualMean = meanOffset + meanSlope * mu`), for the swap
`A' = insert r (A.erase p)`: `meanOffset` for `.inl i` is `-1` at `i = j` and `1` otherwise,
independent of `A`, so it cancels; `meanSlope` is `0/1/-1` by `i in A / i = j / else`, giving
`d(.inl p) = +mu`, `d(.inl r) = -mu`, `d(.inl i) = 0` otherwise. For `.inr (G,h)` with
`c = |G ∩ others A j|`, `others A' j = (others A j) \ {r} ∪ {p}`, so `c - c' = [r in G] - [p in G]`
and `d = (c-c')(1-mu)`. The transposition `tau = (p r)` preserves `Legal` (it fixes `j`,
preserves `|G|` and `j ∉ G`) and negates every `d_e`, so any `tau`-symmetric design has
`S_dv = 0`. All of this is **re-derived by brute-force cube enumeration** in the probe, not
assumed: the affine mean law itself is checked against `world = linear(coeff) + gate` at every
legal measurement, for `q = 2, 3`. For the margin alternative `d_e = meanSlope_e (mu - nu)`
with slopes ranging over `[-q, +1]`, so range `= (1+q)|mu-nu|`, `max|d| = q|mu-nu|`, and
`R = 4q^2/(1+q)^2` by N6.5 — asserted exactly and matched.

**N6.7.** Direct computation on the Boolean cube of `Delta_i` for both fixed worlds.

**Mutation test** (the probe must be able to fail): breaking the completed-square constant by
`1/1000`, replacing the margin factor law `4q^2/(1+q)^2` by `4`, and making the `Legal` filter
vacuously `True` each raise `AssertionError` at the expected line.

## 6. What this does NOT establish

* **It does not establish a factor 4 for anything but the `(0, gamma)` two-design geometry
  with equal `sigma` and equal cost.** With unequal `(sigma, kappa)` the factor is already
  different (6.25 observed). Any sentence of the form "unknown calibration costs a factor 4"
  is false as written and must not be published.
* **It does not establish that 4 is an upper bound.** `(gamma, 9gamma/10)` costs 400 and
  `(gamma, gamma)` costs infinity. A restricted D6 design over the two loudest legal queries
  costs `4q^2`.
* **It does not establish that unknown calibration is free in D6 generally.** `R = 1` is
  proved only for the **swap alternative at fixed margin**, with `sigma_e = 1` (Lean) and
  `kappa_e = 1` (assumed), at `q = 2, 3` and six values of `mu`. For the **margin
  alternative** the cost is `4q^2/(1+q)^2 > 1`, and for **mixed** swap+margin alternatives
  the cancellation demonstrably fails (`R = 1.31`, `1.56` observed).
* **It does not establish anything for `q >= 4`.** The exact D6 sweep was run only at
  `q = 2` and `q = 3`; larger `q` was skipped to stay inside the compute budget, and this is
  stated in the probe header, not silently truncated. The `4q^2/(1+q)^2` and `4q^2` formulas
  are derived and matched at those two `q` only.
* **It does not establish the D6 cost model.** `kappa_e = 1` is this probe's assumption.
  No cost or pricing model for legal queries exists in the D6 Lean source. If cost scales
  with `|G|`, the argmax face changes and every D6 cost factor changes with it.
* **It does not establish that `v_e = 1` is the right offset model for the MASTER `Delta_i`
  readout.** `Delta_i` is already a difference of conditional means; a strictly common
  additive offset on the raw `Y` cancels in it (`v_e = 0`) and there is no nuisance at all.
  The `v == 1` analysis applies to the Lean D6 single-reading query family.
* **It does not prove the blind-minimax corollary in Lean or by exhaustive computation.**
  The claim "blind minimax with an unknown offset equals blind minimax with a known offset"
  rests on a pen-and-paper symmetrization argument over `Sym(coords \ {j})`. What is
  *computed exactly* is the weaker, concrete fact that the uniform-over-singletons design
  has `sum alpha d v = 0` for every swap. No LP was solved; the blind minimax **value** was
  not computed.
* **It does not establish novelty for the identity or the Cauchy–Schwarz optimum.** Both are
  Elfving-1952-era c-optimal design. The brief's prior list omitted Elfving; that omission is
  corrected here, and any paper draft must lead with the citation, not the derivation.
* **It does not treat unknown `v`, multiple nuisances, per-query offsets, drift, unknown
  `sigma_e`, adaptivity, or integer exposure.** All are single-`b`, known-`v`, known-`sigma`,
  continuous-allocation, fixed-design results.
* **It does not certify any Lean theorem.** No `.lean` file was edited, no compile was run,
  and none of the modules read carry an axiom-audited compile receipt.

`(+gamma, -gamma)` pairs, so a reference/control query is a strictly worse design than two
balanced spatial contrasts there, and unknown calibration costs exactly nothing for swap
alternatives at fixed margin while costing exactly `4q^2/(1+q)^2` for margin alternatives.

settle whether the offset attaches before or after the `Delta_i` differencing, extend the
exact sweep past `q = 3`, and get `LeanD6GaussianQueryDominanceV1` and its dependencies an
