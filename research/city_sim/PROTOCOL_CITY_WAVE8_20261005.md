## W8-3 LCD2: the learned committed-duration light with a time-normalised reward (fixes the C-129 design flaw)

- **Change:** as W6-4, with one change. The reward of a decision is −(halting vehicle-seconds over the interval) / (interval length · 10), i.e. the mean halting rate. A per-second discount γ^Δt replaces the per-decision γ, with γ = 0.98 per second and Δt the interval length. Shortening decisions is no longer rewarded per se.
- **Training:** 120 episodes, seeds j0 / j1. DEV gate as registered in W6-4 (LCD alone city TL upper < +0.5 vs RL-H k0 / k1).
- **If the gate passes:** confirmation on 800–819 vs R = the W8-2 fresh lights t14–t18 with the W8-2 fleets (lights unpaired); decision as W6-4.

## W8-4 NET3: a third network (rand3), the whole pipeline with new policies

**Network:** `research/city_sim/net/rand3.net.xml`.
- Built with SUMO `netgenerate --rand` (seed 7, 45 iterations, edges 140–320 m, single lane, 13.89 m/s; TLS guessed, static, opposites layout, 42 / 3 s), then `netconvert --tls.unset` on the junctions whose program had ≠ 2 greens.
- Result: 20 signals, all two-green; 104 edges; mean edge length 231 m; an irregular topology.
- Demand: `randomTrips`, period chosen on TRAIN seed 0 so that the fixed-time city time loss is within ±30 % of grid4's (reported).

**Pipeline (the grid4 recipes, unchanged, with the network patched through `scripts/CITY_n3_run.py` as spider's CITY_m4s_run):**
1. **M1 on rand3:**
   - R10-type PPO fleets s213 / s313 / s413 / s513 trained under FT (400 episodes);
   - DEV check: fleet vs σ\* under FT (fuel, time losses).
2. **Lights:** RL-H t4–t8 on rand3 (Q7b recipe, 120 episodes).
3. **M2 on rand3 (descriptive):** under the learned lights, R (no SPaT) vs the program-derived SPaT ("naive") vs FT benefit.
4. **TSI calibration** per light (traces, open-loop table, closed-loop c2 prep, N2 logs, fit, KF-2 parity, TSI-2 grid / rule on TUNE 660–663).
5. **Confirmation on fresh rand3 traffic 700–719:** lights t4–t8 × fleets × 20; arms R, TSI, HYB, ADP.
   - **Decision (α 0.05):** THIRD-NETWORK REPLICATION PASS iff TSI vs R meets every M3 limit.
   - **Condition (registered):** if the M1 fleet does not save fuel vs σ\* under FT (DEV upper ≥ 0), the network gives no benefit to protect. This is reported, and M2 / TSI are still run descriptively.
