# PROTOCOL W19: is the closed-loop problem created by the signal's design choices? (registered 08/10/2026, 22:25, before any W19 run)

**User request** (08/10, about a reviewer objection the user pasted):
- "Một kiểm chứng mạnh hơn sẽ là so sánh hai signal AI được huấn luyện dưới cùng điều kiện, chỉ khác cách biểu diễn đầu vào … Khi publish SPaT, cả hai có gặp cùng vấn đề không? Signal B có cần adapter không?" (A stronger check: two learned signals trained under the same conditions, differing only in the input representation. When SPaT is published, do both show the same problem? Does signal B need the adapter?)
- "chưa thấy một thí nghiệm chứng minh các mẫu số 10, 20 và 60 là tối ưu" (no experiment shows the denominators 10, 20 and 60 are optimal).
- "việc sử dụng halting count làm reward nó bị đơn giản … halting count chia 10, 10 chắc chắn là m pick random number" (the halting-count reward is simplistic; the 10 was picked arbitrarily).

**The objection to test.** Reviewer form: the authors chose a halting-based signal that is blind to slow vehicles, then built an adapter to fix that weakness, so the problem is artificial.

**Facts at registration:**
- The constants 10, 20 and 60 (observation) and 10 (reward) were fixed by hand as round numbers near typical ranges. They were never derived or tuned.
- The reward −H/10 is a snapshot at the next decision, where H is the halting count (v < 0.1 m/s) on the served lanes of both greens. It shares the observation's 0.1 m/s threshold.
- The loss is the double-DQN Huber loss with κ = 1 (PyTorch `smooth_l1_loss`, default β = 1).

**Seen before registration:**
- W9-6: MaxPressure, a non-learned signal that counts vehicles at every speed. With it, the service alone met the limits and the greens did not lengthen.
- F1: the Salt Lake 13-input light showed no M2 loss. This is confounded with the network and the demand.
- DC-Ada information arm (W12-4): an adapter, not a retrained signal.
- The KW19-0 smoke: the W19 trainer reproduces the RL-H trainer bit for bit.
- Smoke trainings of all ten families (3 episodes) and three single full episodes, used for timing only.
- No W19 evaluation on fresh traffic has run.
- Smoke runs of jobs 104a–104c, before queueing: slot 0, 3-episode lights, seed 860, scratch directory. The pipeline ran end to end, and every self-check passed, KW19-4 included.
  - The smoke lights are untrained: B1H, B2H, B1D, B2D, S2 and R1 switched at every opportunity, so their smoke rows coincide. Smoke numbers carry no evidence.
  - The R100 smoke light almost never switched (0.8% of decisions). Its 300-s traces had no completed green on one phase, so the open-loop forecaster could not be fitted (`KeyError` in `CITY_spat_forecast.rem`), and the job stopped at the prep step. Stage 3 was smoke-tested without R100; R100 uses the AH code path.
- **Rule fixed now:** a fully trained light whose eight traces complete no green on some phase cannot be given a forecaster. The job stops, and that stage is reported as incomplete with this reason (a degenerate signal), not rerun with changes.

## World, fleet, traffic
- **World:** the main grid4 Krauss world, the world in which TSI was developed (not the IDM worlds of W16–W18).
  - W16–W18 changed the car-following model of every car, the fleet's base driver included. They answered a different question (is the fleet's benefit real?), not this one.
- **Fleets:** frozen, the FRESH3 R10 fleets s1413–s1713, trained under FT. The same fleets are used for every signal family.
- **Fresh traffic:** 1400–1419, generated by the job after a non-existence check (KW19-3). Stage 3 uses 1400–1409.
- **Signals:** every family uses the RL-H recipe (`scripts/CITY_w19_train_light.py`, a line-for-line copy of `CITY_q7b_contract.train`).
  - Same hyperparameters: double DQN, din→64→64→2, Huber κ 1, γ 0.9, Adam 1e-3, batch 256, 200 steps per episode, Polyak 0.99, gradient clip 5.
  - Same schedule: 120 episodes of human traffic on TRAIN seeds 0–7, the Q7 ε schedule, Δ = 5 s, minimum green 5 s.
  - **Only the observation, the training reward or one scale constant differs.**
  - Light k of family f uses DQN seed 11 + 100·(34 + j), where j is the slot, shared across families.

**Reward D.** D is summed over the same lanes as H, and a halted vehicle contributes at least 0.993. D therefore has H's unit, so −D/10 adds no new constant. The reward is used only in training; deployed signals see only their observation.

## Arms per light × fleet × traffic (`CITY_w9_run none|em:PHEMlight5 → CITY_w19_run → CITY_w13_modes → evaluator`)

| Arm | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R | Fleet without SPaT (`CITY_eval_f1 --spat-arm none`) |
| SV | Service alone: the per-light hybrid forecast at q 0.1, built by the FRESH3 recipe for this light. The signal's inputs are unchanged. |
| TG | SV plus the light-side adapter at the W15 default (1, 0.25). Fixed in advance, no tuning. Not defined for B2 (no halting feature). For S05 / S2 the adapted count uses the family's own denominator. |
| GATE / OFF | Signal quality: city time loss of light k against FT, every car a SUMO driver (HBEFA4 rows only) |

Every row exists under HBEFA4 and PHEMlight5 (identical trajectories, KM13-1a/1b).

**Forecaster per light (FRESH3 recipe):**
1. 8 traces (σ 0.3, all SUMO cars) → open-loop table.
2. Closed-loop c2 prep with the development fleet (`CITY_w19_prep.py`). Its `LightE` sees the family's observation.
3. 8 N2 logs → N2 fit.
4. Checks: KF-3 for every light; KF-2 for the first light of each family.

## Decisions (crossed bootstrap, B 2000; lights × fleets × traffic)

"Meets every limit" uses the TSI limits:
- agent fuel upper < 0 under HBEFA4 **and** PHEMlight5;
- city, agent and human time-loss uppers < +0.5 s/veh;
- teleports ≤ R + 0.1 per run, unfinished ≤ R.

**Stage 1 (job 104a; families B1H, B2H against AH).** Each at α 0.025, one-sided 97.5%, Bonferroni over the two families.
- **D19-1[f]:** SV − R meets every limit, i.e. signal f needs no light-side adapter.
- **D19-2[f]:** the DiD in agent time loss, (SV − R)_f − (SV − R)_AH, has upper < 0, i.e. the service harms f's fleet less than RL-H's.
- **R19-A (replication, descriptive, 95%):** for AH, SV − R fails the limits and TG − R meets them (the earlier pattern).

**Stage 2 (job 104b; AD, B1D, B2D against AH).** The same D19-1 and D19-2, each at α 0.05/3.

**Stage 3 (job 104c; S05, S2, R1, R100; traffic 1400–1409).** Descriptive at 95%:
- does the AH pattern recur (SV harms, TG meets)?
- the agent-time DiD against AH, with a two-sided 95% CI.

**Reported for every family (descriptive):**
- light gate: GATE − FT city time loss, one-sided 95%;
- TG − R and TG − SV;
- mechanism: mean green and served-lane halting under R and SV.

A family whose gate fails (not better than FT) is reported as a weak signal; its comparison stands but carries less weight.

## Reading map, fixed now

| Outcome | Reading |
|---|---|
| D19-1 SHOWN for B1 / B2 | The light-side half of TSI is needed because of the halting-count observation, as Theorem `thm:twosided` item 6 and W9-6 predict. The paper must say the problem is specific to halting-count (threshold) signals, and TSI's light half is a repair for those signals, not for learned signals in general. |
| D19-1 NOT SHOWN for B with a clear harm | The closed loop is not an artifact of the halting feature: the distribution shift caused by gliding fleets hurts learned signals with richer inputs too. |
| Stage 2 (reward) | The same reading for the reward's threshold. |
| Stage 3 (constants) | If the pattern recurs, the finding does not depend on the hand-picked constants. If it does not, the paper must report the dependence. |

**Not claimed:**
- Optimality of any constant (none was tuned, and W19 does not tune them).
- Other DQN hyperparameters, network sizes, or signal families beyond keep / switch.
