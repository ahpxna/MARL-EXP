\section*{50. SCOPE RESTRICTION on what Sections 48--49 establish}
\claimbox{The certificate intervals in this experiment are constructed \emph{from the truth}. Therefore these tables are a \textbf{utility and tightening} result --- how often each $\Gamma$ yields a usable certificate, and how tight it is --- and are \textbf{not} statistical coverage evidence. \texttt{false\_safe\_rate} $=0$ here means the construction never emitted an unsafe certificate \emph{given the truth}, not that a deployed estimator would achieve nominal coverage. \eclass{DEVELOPMENT\_EMPIRICAL}}
This restriction is stated because the sign-stability across three seeds makes the table look stronger than it is; three seeds establish reproducibility of a construction, not calibration of an estimator. \texttt{milp\_status} remains \textsf{NOT\_IMPLEMENTED}; exact enumeration and LP are reported separately and no non-optimal solver status is ever read as a lower bound.

\section*{52. A ledger-reading defect, and the rule it produces}
Two sections of tonight's addenda were written wrong and corrected before compile, both from the same cause: \textbf{a result artifact was read without its companion status records.} Report 4 quoted a \texttt{next\_formal\_target} field that a \texttt{*.FORMAL\_TARGET\_MET.json} companion had already marked \textsf{MET}, understating the Structural chain by a full evidence class; Report 5 declared the $m$-generalisation unprobed when \texttt{TangentSeed\_m\{4,5,6,7\}} had run on 6 September. The companions exist precisely because result artifacts are hash-pinned and must never be edited in place, so a stale field inside one is expected and is \emph{not} a defect of the artifact --- it is a defect of the reader.

\claimbox{\textbf{Rule.} Before quoting any \texttt{next\_formal\_target}, \texttt{status} or ``open'' field from a result artifact, enumerate the companion status records that annotate it. Before reporting that a direction was never tried, enumerate the artifacts for that direction by name pattern, not by memory of the session.}
