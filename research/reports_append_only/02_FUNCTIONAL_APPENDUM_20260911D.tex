\textbf{Append-only rule.} Sections 1--66, including addenda A--C, are an immutable prefix. This addendum begins at Section 67.

\section*{67. The endpoint was undefined, and the artifact said so}
The $300$-instance allocation run reported \texttt{deployable\_winner\_status = "NO\_MEDIAN\_IDENTIFIED\_CENSORING"} with the winner field literally \texttt{null}: every variant's median sample count to certificate sat at $16385=\max_n+1$. Certificate fractions ($40.0\%$ for \texttt{C\_anytime} against $21.7\%$ for \texttt{uniform}) were, by the artifact's own censoring contract, secondary diagnostics. The primary endpoint did not exist in that run.

\section*{68. Raising the budget identifies it}
Re-running at $\max_n=65536$ ($4\times$):

\texttt{deployable\_winner\_status} becomes \texttt{IDENTIFIED\_BY\_MEDIAN}, winner \texttt{C\_anytime}. Every variant gains $15$--$31$ percentage points; nothing saturates.
\claimbox{\texttt{C\_anytime} reaches a certificate in a median $39\,484$ samples against \texttt{uniform}'s $>65\,537$, hence \textbf{at least $1.66\times$ faster} --- a \emph{lower} bound on the advantage, since \texttt{uniform} is still censored --- while sitting within $\mathbf{1.0\%}$ of the known-$\sigma$ oracle. \texttt{false\_safe\_count} is $0$ for all six. \eclass{DEVELOPMENT EXPERIMENT}, one seed, $100$ instances.}

\section*{69. A retraction}
This session earlier argued from the censored distribution that the non-certifying $60\%$ might be \emph{structurally uncertifiable} rather than merely slow --- the certified-$N$ quantiles looked bimodal, with medians around $7\,000$ among certifiers --- and concluded that raising $\max_n$ might not produce a median at all.
\claimbox{\textbf{That was wrong, and wrong by a familiar mechanism}: extrapolating a shape from a censored sample with no model of the process. In a stationary Gaussian design, once every cell is sampled enough the interval contracts and a certificate appears; the correct reading was that the budget, not the population, was the binding constraint. Recorded because the same error --- reading a truncated view as if it were the whole --- has now occurred several times in this chain.}

\section*{70. What is still required}
One seed and $100$ instances is not a confirmatory result, and \texttt{uniform} remains censored, so the $1.66\times$ figure is a floor rather than a measurement. The confirmatory run is $300$ instances across three seeds at $\max_n=131072$, which should also uncensor \texttt{uniform} and turn the ratio into a number. Certification rate as a function of budget remains the honest secondary curve to report alongside the median.
