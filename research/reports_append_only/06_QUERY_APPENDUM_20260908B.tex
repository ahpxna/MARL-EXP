\section*{77. NOVELTY RELOCATION: the transfer matrix is a deficiency measurement}
A prior-art check places this chain adjacent to \emph{sufficiency and deficiency} theory in the sense of Blackwell and Le Cam. Comparing what one method--query pair can answer against what another can, on a common state and a common intervention panel, is a comparison of experiments; the quantity the transfer matrix reports is a deficiency, measured in one simulator under one budget.

\claimbox{The chain may claim: a typed, capability-gated, budget-matched \emph{instrument} for measuring deficiency between relational query methods in multi-agent simulators, with a non-degeneracy gate that refuses to aggregate constant targets. It may \emph{not} claim a new theory of query sufficiency. The blocked-method and degenerate-cell bookkeeping is the contribution; the comparison principle is not.}
