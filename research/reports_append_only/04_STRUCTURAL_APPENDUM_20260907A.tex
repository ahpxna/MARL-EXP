\section*{60. DEMOTION: the confirmation instance family was too easy}
The three-seed run (\texttt{kprefix\_three\_level\_tierA\_v1}, $m=6$, $K\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, seeds $8201$--$8203$) reproduced: exact and greedy reach worst-budget regret $0$ from $K=2$ on $36/36$ worlds, and the learned network is worse on all three seeds. \oldclaim{Exact and greedy reach zero regret from two menus, so two menus suffice for budget-independent ranking on this family.} The measurement is real; the \emph{inference} is not available, because $34/36$ of those worlds are already solved at $K=1$. A family in which almost every instance is solvable with one menu cannot demonstrate the benefit of two. \eclass{DEMOTED: reproducible but uninformative; the family, not the algorithm, is the confound}

\section*{62. Why this is the family the chain needed}
The prefix-cover dimension is \emph{known by construction}, so for every $r\ge3$ the family supplies instances that provably cannot be solved with two menus. That is exactly the property the $36$-world family lacked. The correct comparison --- exact versus greedy versus learned at $K<\chi$, $K=\chi$, $K>\chi$ --- is now well posed for the first time, with a ground truth to be right or wrong against.

\section*{62. CORRECTION: this law is already PROVED, parametrically}
\textbf{The run above is a re-verification, not a discovery, and the first draft of this section said otherwise.} The companion record
\texttt{staircase\_\allowbreak exact\_\allowbreak r7\_\allowbreak 20260904.\allowbreak FORMAL\_\allowbreak TARGET\_\allowbreak MET.json}
(recorded 5 September, \eclass{PROVED}) states:
\[\chi_{\text{prefix}}(F_r,0)=r=m/2\qquad\text{for every }r>0,\]
proved parametrically in \texttt{Lean\allowbreak Structural\allowbreak Staircase\allowbreak Exact\allowbreak PrefixCoverV1}, compile errors $0$, axiom audit present. The status of this family moved
\begin{center}\scriptsize\textsf{EXACT\_FINITE\_THEOREM\_DISCOVERY}\\$\longrightarrow$\\\textsf{PROVED (parametric in }$r$)\end{center}
on 5 September, and its stated consequence is that \emph{``the Structural chain no longer rests on a $\Theta(m)$ bound plus finite computation.''}

Tonight's $r=1..6$ computation agrees with the proved law at every tested $r$ and is therefore a consistency check of the instrument, not new evidence about $\chi$.

\textbf{Why the mistake happened, recorded so it is not repeated.} The result artifact still carries the field
\begin{quote}\scriptsize\texttt{next\_formal\_target}: ``construct $r$ rankings for every $r$ and prove \textsf{PrefixCoverDimensionAtMost} staircase $0$ $r$''\end{quote}
and that field was quoted verbatim as an open target. It is not open: the companion record marks it \textsf{MET}. The artifact was not edited, deliberately --- \emph{``rewriting \texttt{next\_formal\_target} in place would invalidate the manifest hash and destroy the record of what the artifact claimed when it was produced.''} \textbf{The lesson is that a result artifact's own ``next target'' field is not authoritative; the companion status records are.} Read \texttt{*.FORMAL\_TARGET\_MET.json} before quoting any \texttt{next\_formal\_target}.

\section*{63. What is actually still open}
Verbatim from the companion record's \texttt{still\_open}: \emph{``This is the law for the staircase family $F_r$ specifically. It is NOT a minimax statement over arbitrary relation families, and nothing here claims one.''}

So the open question is not the parametric upper bound --- that is closed --- but whether any relation family forces $\chi$ above $m/2$, i.e.\ whether the staircase is extremal. The value of tonight's run is unchanged by the correction and lies entirely in Section 60: it supplies instances of \emph{known} $\chi$, so an exact-vs-greedy-vs-learned comparison at $K<\chi$, $K=\chi$, $K>\chi$ is now well posed against a proved ground truth rather than against a family where $34/36$ instances are trivial.

\claimbox{\textbf{Evidence classes, corrected.} $\chi_{\text{prefix}}(F_r,0)=r$: \eclass{PROVED, parametric, axiom-audited, 5 September}. Tonight's $r=1..6$ table: \eclass{consistency check}. The $36$-world confirmation: \eclass{reproducible but uninformative}.}
