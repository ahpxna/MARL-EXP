\section*{81. The first evaluable cells}
Every configuration reported through Section 80 returned $0$ evaluable cells against $336$ degenerate, at $h=1,4,8$ and under both continuation policies. A positive control built on twelve pre-constructed delivery states changes that: \textbf{$336$ cells become regret-evaluable}, while the reset/warm-up branch remains at $0$.
\claimbox{\textbf{What this establishes and what it does not.} It establishes that the measurement path carries signal once the state affords a delivery opportunity --- so the instrument is not broken, and the earlier all-zero result was a property of the state distribution, not of the metric. It does \emph{not} establish transfer on natural trajectories, which is the claim the chain needs. \eclass{POSITIVE CONTROL}, not evidence for the transfer hypothesis.}
This also revises the working diagnosis recorded earlier. Raising the horizon was tried at $h=1,4,8$ and failed at every setting; the binding constraint was the state distribution. The 7 September plan had already located the issue in that region --- it argued that cells which are summary-insufficient must be observed rather than filtered --- and the present control is consistent with that reading.

\section*{82. Module recorded}
\texttt{Lean\allowbreak Adaptive\allowbreak Gaussian\allowbreak Query\allowbreak NoiseV1}, four names: \texttt{query\_noise\_normalization}, \texttt{selected\_increment}, \texttt{count\_slice\_tail}, \texttt{normalized\_exponential\_tail}. Audited, no \texttt{sorryAx}. These supply tail control for query noise under adaptive selection; they are infrastructure for the transfer measurement, not a transfer result.

\section*{83. What the chain still lacks}
A panel with a verified non-constant target on natural trajectories, an optimal decoder solved on small instances so that information limits are separated from decoder error, and a budget sweep before any scale-up. Until those exist, the transfer claim has a positive control and no confirmatory evidence. The identifiability lab ($5000$ instances, \texttt{insufficient\_fraction} $=0.5052$, \texttt{mean\_minimax\_floor} $=0.4218$) supports the theoretical core and is explicitly scoped away from natural-language routing; it is synthetic and does not substitute for runtime evidence.
