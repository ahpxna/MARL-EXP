\section*{78. MASTER's own theoretical gap, stated precisely}
\texttt{CertificateContract.sound} is a \emph{field of the structure}:
\[(\forall o\in\mathrm{required},\ \mathrm{obligationHolds}\,o)\to\mathrm{regret}\,\mathrm{selected}\le\varepsilon.\]
The implication from evidence to regret is therefore supplied, not derived, and the remaining mathematical content of the completion module is minimum-cost selection over a finite set. The chain has now produced a \emph{concrete} contract whose \texttt{sound} is proved from the geometry rather than assumed --- which is the correct first step, and reorders the work: a contract with proved soundness for at least one CIG family must precede any acquisition-cost theorem.

\section*{79. A negative result worth keeping}
The held-out probe (\texttt{master\_reference\_heldout}) shows that on the small instances tested, computing the certificate makes total runtime \emph{slower} than solving the problem exactly. \eclass{DEVELOPMENT EXPERIMENT}, and a negative one.
\claimbox{Kept deliberately. It bounds where the certified route can pay at all, and it is the kind of result that quietly disappears from a programme that only records successes. The regime question --- at what problem size, and under what evidence-reuse structure, certification becomes cheaper than exact solution --- is now a stated target rather than an assumption.}
