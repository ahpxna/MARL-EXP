\section*{66. New result of the day, cross-chain}
A margin-sensitive exchange bound is \eclass{PROVED} for every $m$, with no symmetry hypothesis and no fixed active cell:
\[2\bigl[L_F(S)-L_F(T)\bigr]\le d\,(2\delta_\square-\mu),\qquad d=|S\setminus T|.\]
Composed with exactly certified lower bounds it gives $R_6(\mu)=6-3\mu$ at five values of $\mu$ and $R_8(0)=8$, upper and lower bound coinciding. This is the first quantity in the project with a proved upper bound and matching exact witnesses across a parameter, and it is the reason D6 is now the lead chain.
