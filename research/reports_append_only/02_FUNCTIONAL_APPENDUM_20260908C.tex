\section*{65. CORRECTION: update B over-demoted the chain}
Addendum B, Section 63, concluded from $360$ runs that certification is governed by \texttt{true\_topk\_gap} ($r=+0.68$) and recorded:
\begin{center}\scriptsize\oldclaim{DEMOTED: headline confounded; the measurement stands, the attribution does not}\end{center}
The correlation is real and unchanged. \textbf{The inference that allocation does not matter was wrong}, and the reason is a design point update B did not exploit: the six policies run on \emph{the same instances under common random streams}, so a within-instance paired comparison isolates policy from gap. \textbf{$55$ instances where anytime certifies and uniform does not; zero in the reverse direction; the advantage appears on all three seeds.} Because the comparison is paired at fixed gap, gap cannot account for the difference between policies. Pilot and shrink now differ in allocation on $299/300$ instances, and the single agreeing instance certified immediately after the pilot phase.

\claimbox{\textbf{Corrected conclusion.} Gap governs difficulty; \emph{anytime improves the probability of certification within the budget tested}. Median sample complexity remains undetermined --- every variant is still censored at $\max n+1$ --- and no optimality is claimed. \eclass{NUMERICAL EVIDENCE, three seeds, paired within instance}}

\section*{66. Novelty is still not established by this}
Neither ``small gap is hard'' nor adding a shrinkage step is a contribution. Pure exploration with multiple correct answers already has matching lower bounds and asymptotically optimal allocation, and replacing the finite-horizon Bonferroni bound with a proper anytime confidence sequence is a \emph{necessary baseline}, not a result. The chain's current advantage is measured against a deliberately conservative bound.

\textbf{Replacement direction.} Hold the Top-$k$ gap and the noise fixed, and vary the \emph{distance between the extremal actions and the competing action}. Measure how the extremal structure moves certification cost, then look for an allocation and a matching bound that exploit that structure. The present runner pins the extrema to the first and last action and generates noise shared across actions within a relation; that structure must be diversified before any general claim, because as written the instance family cannot distinguish a structural effect from an artefact of its own construction.
