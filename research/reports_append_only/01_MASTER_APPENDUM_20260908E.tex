\section*{73. Checking names against the pinned Mathlib, before compiling}
D6 Section 151 recorded that \texttt{Lean\allowbreak D6\allowbreak Information\allowbreak LimitV1} used Mathlib lemma names \emph{as recalled} and unverified. Grepping the pinned copy in \texttt{.lake/packages/mathlib} found three defects. All are now fixed; the recalled draft is preserved beside the module as \texttt{.recall\_draft\_20260908}, deleted nothing.
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{0.98\columnwidth}{X X}
\toprule
Defect & Resolution\\
\midrule
\texttt{Nat.succ\_mul\_choose\_eq} \textbf{does not exist} in the pinned Mathlib & renamed upstream to \texttt{Nat.add\_one\_mul\_choose\_eq} (\texttt{Data/Nat/Choose/Basic}~131), same statement $(n{+}1)\binom nk=\binom{n+1}{k+1}(k{+}1)$\\
\texttt{choose\_shift} closed by \texttt{omega} & \textbf{unsound choice}: the goal $(2j{+}1)\binom{2j}{j+1}=j\binom{2j+1}{j+1}$ is a product of two \emph{variables}, and \texttt{omega} decides linear arithmetic only. Replaced by an explicit additive cancellation through \texttt{Nat.add\_left\_cancel}\\
\texttt{Finset.card\_filter} assumed to be in \texttt{Data/Finset/Card} & it exists, but in \texttt{Algebra/BigOperators/Group/Finset/Piecewise}~278, and the summand orientation is \texttt{ite (p i) 1 0}; the calc steps were restated in that orientation so both uses share one lemma shape\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
Seven further names were confirmed present with the expected statements: \texttt{sdiff\_eq\_filter}, \texttt{card\_powersetCard}, \texttt{mem\_powersetCard}, \texttt{subset\_erase}, \texttt{card\_erase\_of\_mem}, \texttt{powersetCard\_nonempty}, \texttt{exists\_max\_image} (whose conclusion is $\exists x\in s,\ \forall x'\in s,\ f x'\le f x$, matching the destructuring used). \texttt{Nat.mul\_le\_mul\_left}, whose signature has moved between versions, was replaced by the stable \texttt{Nat.mul\_le\_mul}.
\claimbox{\textbf{Method, added to the standing notes.} A source file quoting lemma names from memory is not a proof proposal, it is a guess. Grepping the pinned dependency costs one command and caught an \emph{unsound tactic choice} that no amount of re-reading the mathematics would have surfaced --- \texttt{omega} on a nonlinear goal fails at compile time, but the failure is about the instrument, not the claim. This check now precedes every hand-off of unfamiliar Lean.}

All $2^7=128$ subsets were scanned for each budget; at every budget exactly one set meets the bound, and it is the designated optimum. The five optima are pairwise non-nested, so they form an antichain and $\chi_{\mathrm{prefix}}\ge5>7/2$.
\claimbox{\textbf{What this does and does not settle.} The promotion is now a pure \emph{tactic} question: the arithmetic is confirmed true, so if kernel \texttt{decide} discharges it the claim becomes \eclass{PROVED}, and if \texttt{decide} exceeds its limits the claim stays \eclass{COMPILED ARTIFACT} --- but it will not turn out to be \emph{false}. This enumeration is itself only \eclass{NUMERICAL EVIDENCE} and licenses nothing on its own; its role is to tell us which kind of failure to expect.}

\section*{75. If kernel \texttt{decide} does not fit}
Recorded now so the fallback is not invented under pressure. Direct \texttt{decide} scans $128$ subsets, and the expensive part is not the arithmetic ($896$ integer additions) but forcing \texttt{Finset.univ} on \texttt{Finset (Fin 7)}, a powerset with de-duplication. The cheaper decomposition uses \texttt{Finset.card\_eq\_one}, \texttt{card\_eq\_two}, \texttt{card\_eq\_three}: $7+21+35=63$ cases for budgets $1,2,3$. Mathlib has no \texttt{card\_eq\_four} or \texttt{card\_eq\_five}, so budgets $4$ and $5$ must go through the complement, whose cardinalities are $3$ and $2$ --- which suits this witness, since \texttt{complementAbs7} is defined on the complement already.
\textbf{This fallback is not written.} Shipping a second, more elaborate proof that also cannot be compiled here would raise the failure probability, not lower it. It gets written when the first attempt's error message says it is needed.
