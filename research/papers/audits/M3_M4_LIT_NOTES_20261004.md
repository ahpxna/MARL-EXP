# W4 literature notes for M3 / M4 positioning (21:25, 04/10/2026; only sources whose existence was checked by web search tonight)

| Source (verified) | What it does | Consequence for our claims |
|---|---|---|
| Bodenheimer, Brauer, Eckhoff, German, "Enabling GLOSA for adaptive traffic lights", IEEE VNC 2014; follow-up "GLOSA for Adaptive Traffic Lights: Methods and Evaluation", 2015 (FAU CRIS) | SPaT for adaptive controllers from the controller's degrees of freedom (graph transformation) | GLOSA under adaptive control with predicted SPaT is NOT new. Our forecast arms (M2b, F1 provider) are instances; no novelty claim for them. |
| Mahler & Vahidi, "An optimal velocity-planning scheme for vehicle energy efficiency through probabilistic prediction of traffic-signal timing", IEEE T-ITS 2014 | Probabilistic SPaT (historical + real-time phase), then optimal velocity | Planning on probabilistic timing is known. F1-scratch is a LEARNED fleet adapted to one learned light's empirical timing distribution; at most "a retrained fleet recovers the benefit". It is a deployment finding, not a method contribution. |
| SAE J2735 SPaT fields minEndTime / likelyTime / maxEndTime / confidence (as described in the PMC paper on SPaT prediction at actuated signals, PMC11944547) | Standard message semantics for uncertain timing | Our "commitment" (W-c, RPC) is a minEndTime = maxEndTime guarantee. The M3-NC negative concerns the COST of making a learned light's timing certain, not the message format. |
| arXiv 2208.13709, "Optimization of Vehicle Trajectories Considering Uncertainty in Actuated Traffic Signal Timings" | Vehicle-side robustness to timing uncertainty | This supports the reading that the adaptation burden can sit on the vehicle side (F1). It is compatible with the M3 light-side negative; it is not evidence for it. |

**Boundaries for the paper (to be kept):**
- (i) Do not claim novelty for GLOSA under adaptive lights, probabilistic SPaT, or commitment messages.
- (ii) Our contribution candidates are empirical and conditional:
  - the compatibility loss / harm when a learned light replaces a static one (M2, replicated on spider, C-81);
  - the confirmed cost of light-side certainty (M3-NC, pending);
  - the retrained-fleet reference (F1-A1, pending).
- (iii) Cooperative signal–vehicle RL (e.g. CoTV, already in the corpus) trains both populations jointly. Our staged-upgrade setting with a frozen population is the distinction. Write it as a setting, not as a novelty of "two populations".
